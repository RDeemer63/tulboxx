🔄 Format Flexibility: Multiple Estimate Styles Built In
We’ve built TULBOXX to accommodate different customer types and business styles. The user can choose between:

Single Price Estimates
Ideal for residential clients who want clarity and simplicity. We use value-based language and a polished project description to show what’s included and why it’s worth the price.

Line Item Estimates
Great for clients who want to see a breakdown. Users can list out each part of the job (labor, materials, disposal, etc.), with editable descriptions and pricing per item.

Categorized Estimates
For more complex projects, users can group line items into sections like "Site Prep," "Demo," "Install," and "Cleanup" to create a clean, easy-to-follow structure.

Each format can be enhanced with:

AI-generated language that sounds confident and clear

Attached photos, drawings, or files

Editable terms and conditions for legal clarity
