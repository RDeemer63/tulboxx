# Tulboxx CRM — Must-Have Feature Checklist  
_Comprehensive list prioritized by business impact & user adoption (Service-based industries)_

---

## Tier 1 · Mission-Critical (Launch Blockers)
| # | Feature | Why It Matters | Business Impact |
|---|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Unified Customer & Lead Database | Single source of truth; no duplicate entry. | Faster onboarding, data integrity |
| 2 | Estimate → Job → Invoice Workflow | Core revenue path; must flow in ≤ 7 clicks. | Directly ties to cashflow |
| 3 | E-Signature & Acceptance Tracking | 60-80 % higher close rate vs. print/scan. | Shortens sales cycle, clear audit trail |
| 4 | Drag-and-Drop Scheduling & Crew Allocation | Rescheduling is daily reality; friction here kills adoption. | Operational efficiency, reduces phone calls |
| 5 | Mobile Field App (Offline-First) | Techs spend day on site, not at desks. | Accurate time/notes capture, happier crews |
| 6 | Real-Time Communication Hub (Email/SMS) | Customers expect status updates & reminders. | Reduces no-shows, boosts satisfaction |
| 7 | Secure JWT Auth + RBAC | Protect sensitive data, comply with regulations. | Trust & liability protection |
| 8 | Robust Reporting & Dashboards | Owners need visibility on revenue, utilization. | Data-driven decisions, upsell insight |
| 9 | Automated Backups & Disaster Recovery | Data loss = business death. | Business continuity |
